## ❓ About the Project

Rosen Ritter is a centralized collection of Open-Source Cyber Threat Intelligence (OSCTI), bringing together Indicators of Compromise (IOCs) and Indicators of Attack (IOAs) from multiple independent threat intelligence providers, security research teams, and community-driven feeds.

- 🎯 Makes threat intelligence **accessible, organized and integration-ready**
- 👥 Built for **security professionals, SOC teams, researchers** and defensive security operations
- 🔗 Consolidates intelligence from different sources into **one centralized collection**
- 🛠️ Supports **threat hunting, detection engineering, incident response** and proactive defence

---

## 🔎 Intelligence Sources

The collection aggregates intelligence from established security research teams and community-driven platforms, including:

- **CrowdStrike Counter Adversary Operations**
- **Palo Alto Networks Unit 42**
- **URLhaus**
- **ThreatFox**
- **ThreatBase**
- **MISP** communities
- Additional open-source threat intelligence feeds

---

## 📡 Indicator Coverage

Rossen Ritter collects and distributes multiple types of Indicators of Compromise (IOCs) and Indicators of Attack (IOAs):

| Indicator Type | Description |
|:---|:---|
| **IPv4 / IPv6** | Malicious, suspicious and potentially benign IP addresses |
| **Domains** | Domains associated with malicious infrastructure |
| **URLs** | Malware distribution, phishing and malicious URLs |
| **MD5** | File hashes for malware identification |
| **SHA1** | File hashes for threat correlation |
| **SHA256** | File hashes for threat identification and investigation |

> [!NOTE]
> IP intelligence may include active threats, historical indicators and benign addresses, depending on the original intelligence source and its classification.

## ⚙️ Automated Collection & Distribution

Threat intelligence collection and distribution are automated through the **Armored-PCs Threat Intelligence Platform**.

The platform handles:

- Automated feed collection
- Indicator processing and organization
- Consolidation from multiple intelligence providers
- Automated list generation and distribution
- Periodic intelligence refresh

⏱️ **Update Frequency:** Every 6 hours

## 🔗 Integration & Use Cases

Rossen Ritter is designed to support integration into existing cybersecurity environments.

- **SIEM** — Threat detection and correlation
- **XDR / EDR** — Indicator-based detection and investigation
- **SOAR** — Automated enrichment and response workflows
- **Firewalls** — IP and domain-based blocking policies
- **Threat Hunting** — Proactive investigation of suspicious activity
- **MISP** — Threat intelligence correlation and sharing

## 💡 Project Philosophy

Like a knight guarding what matters, Rosen Ritter represents the continuous collection, organization and sharing of intelligence to help defend digital infrastructure.
We believe cybersecurity should not be a privilege reserved for large enterprises or organizations with extensive security teams. Every team, business and organization deserves the awareness, knowledge and tools needed to better protect its digital environment.

Our mission is to help security teams and organizations strengthen their defensive capabilities through accessible threat intelligence, automation and security awareness.

We believe that effective automation, alerting and intelligent triage can significantly reduce the impact of security threats by helping teams identify, prioritize and respond to a large portion of security issues more efficiently. By reducing repetitive manual tasks and improving visibility, security professionals can focus their efforts where they matter most.

But technology alone is not enough.

We also want to raise awareness among those who believe cybersecurity is not for everyone. Security is a shared responsibility, not just the job of a dedicated IT or SOC department. Every organization, regardless of its size or technical maturity, has something worth protecting.
Together, they embody a simple philosophy:

> **Knowledge is the first line of defence. Intelligence is the foundation of protection.**
>
> Our goal is not simply to collect indicators of compromise, but to help build a more informed, prepared and resilient security community.

Because cybersecurity is not just for the few. It is for everyone.
