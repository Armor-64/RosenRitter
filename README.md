The Knight of the Rose

An Open-Source Threat Intelligence Collection by ArmoredPCS

Knowledge is the first line of defence.

A centralized collection of open-source Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI) feeds, bringing together indicators from multiple independent threat intelligence providers.

Rossen Ritter aims to make threat intelligence accessible, organized and integration-ready for security professionals, SOC teams, researchers and defensive security operations.

Collect · Correlate · Investigate · Defend

Maintained by ArmoredPCS.

Project Philosophy

Like a knight guarding what matters, Rossen Ritter represents the continuous collection and sharing of intelligence to help defend digital infrastructure.

The rose symbolizes vigilance, resilience and the responsibility of protecting what matters.
